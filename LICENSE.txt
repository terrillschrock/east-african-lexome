─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
NOTE ON DUAL LICENSING WITH RWC
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

EALex's semantic domain tagging uses the SIL Rapid Word Collection
(RWC) hierarchy, which is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The CC BY-SA
license is "copyleft": adaptations of RWC material must themselves be
released under CC BY-SA 4.0.

EALex handles this in the following way:

  - The EALex dataset as a whole is licensed CC BY 4.0.

  - EALex uses RWC domain IDs as reference annotations in the
    Semantic_Field column of the MeaningTable. Referencing the
    RWC hierarchy via its identifiers (a use of the work) is
    distinguished from adapting the hierarchy itself.

  - If EALex develops extensions to the RWC hierarchy (additional
    domains or subdomains tuned for East African vocabulary
    that RWC does not currently cover), those extensions will be
    published as separately-licensed artifacts under CC BY-SA 4.0,
    kept distinct from the main CC BY 4.0 dataset and clearly
    labeled as such.

This arrangement preserves CC BY 4.0 as the license under which
EALex's lexical data is distributed, while honoring the copyleft
terms of the RWC hierarchy on which the domain-tagging methodology
depends.
